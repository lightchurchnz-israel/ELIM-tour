Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

ELIM TOUR respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through our website or booking links is handled.

Information you provide

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, travel dates and trip preferences. Our current website inquiry form prepares an email on your device; it does not claim that a message has been sent until you send that email.

Bókun bookings

Tour availability, booking and payment are handled through Bókun. Information submitted in the Bókun booking interface is subject to the privacy and payment terms presented in that booking flow.

How we use information

We use information you send to answer enquiries, arrange tours, provide booked services and communicate necessary trip information. We do not sell customer information.
